Let's first prove that (∀ x : A, ¬ P x) → ¬ (∃ x : A, P x)
Assume na : ∀ x : A, ¬ P x
Assume ne : ∃ x : A, P x
By ne we get a such that ha : P a
Claim npa : ¬ P a from na applied to a
We conclude by npa applied to ha
Let's now prove that ¬ (∃ x : A, P x) → ∀ x : A, ¬ P x
Assume ne : ¬ (∃ x : A, P x)
Fix a : A
Assume pa : P a
We apply ne
Let's prove that a works
We conclude by pa
